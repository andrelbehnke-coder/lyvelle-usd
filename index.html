Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Is this really a limited promotion?
Yes. Dyson almost never discounts the Airwrap, and the Spring Edition release of $129 (from $597) is available only while stock lasts — typically a matter of hours. Once the allocation is gone, the next markdown is uncertain.
Is it suitable for my hair type?
Every texture — fine to coily, straight to curly, colour-treated or natural. The six attachments adapt to different densities, and intelligent heat control protects more fragile hair automatically.
How is it different from a curling or flat iron?
Traditional tools rely on direct contact heat, often above 400°F, which breaks down hair proteins over time. The Airwrap styles with high-velocity airflow via the Coanda effect, capped at 302°F — so there is never a hot plate against your strand.
What's the warranty and return policy?
A full 2-year Dyson warranty covering parts and labour, plus a 30-day unconditional return window. If it isn't right for you, we arrange collection and refund — no questions, no restocking fees.
When will my order arrive?
Orders placed before 3pm GMT ship the same day via complimentary 2-day delivery anywhere in the United Kingdom. A tracking link is emailed the moment your Airwrap leaves our distribution centre.
